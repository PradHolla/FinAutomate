# REPORT

Design write-up. Target length 1 to 3 pages.

The seven headings below are prescribed by the assignment (Section 6.2). Do not
rename or reorder them.

## 1. Architecture

_Architecture and the key decisions, plus trade-offs._

## 2. Artifact schema

_The schema and why it is shaped that way._

## 3. Determinism & error handling

_How replay is made deterministic. How runtime errors and exceptional states are
detected and handled. Secondarily, UI drift._

## 4. Heterogeneity & multi-tenant

_How the design extends to legacy web and desktop surfaces, and to reuse across
institutions running the same app._

## 5. Escalation & handoff

_How "stuck" is detected, how a human takes control of the live session, and how
control is handed back._

## 6. Safety

_The guardrail model and its limits._

## 7. Cuts

_What was deliberately left out, and what would come next._
